👻 The Ghost That Wore Her Face

🌿 African Folktale • 🕒 26 min read

👻 Chapter One – The Woman in the Glass

Lagos was never truly quiet.

Cars filled the roads every morning.

People hurried to work.

Street traders called out to customers.

Music floated from buses and small shops.

The city never seemed to sleep.

Ada had lived there all her life.

She worked in a small fashion shop where she sewed clothes for people.

Everyone liked Ada.

She smiled often.

She greeted everyone.

She never liked trouble.

Every evening after work, she walked home through the same streets.

It was quicker than waiting for a bus.

One rainy evening, the whole city looked different.

The roads were wet.

Street lights reflected on the water like tiny stars.

Most people had already gone home.

Ada opened her umbrella and continued walking.

As she passed an old building, she noticed something strange.

A broken shop window stood beside the road.

She looked into the glass without thinking.

She saw herself.

Or...

She thought she did.

The woman inside the glass looked exactly like her.

Same face.

Same clothes.

Same hairstyle.

But something was wrong.

The woman was smiling.

Ada wasn't.

Her heart skipped.

She quickly stepped away from the window.

When she looked again...

The reflection was normal.

She laughed softly.

"I must be tired."

She continued walking.

When she reached home, her younger brother was watching television.

Her mother was already serving dinner.

Everything felt normal again.

She forgot about the strange reflection.

That night...

Ada woke up suddenly.

Someone had whispered her name.

"Ada..."

She quickly sat up.

The room was dark.

Everyone else was asleep.

She listened carefully.

Nothing.

She shook her head.

"It was only a dream."

She lay down again.

The next morning she stood before the mirror while getting ready for work.

She adjusted her hair.

She smiled.

The mirror smiled back.

Then...

The reflection slowly stopped smiling.

Ada was still smiling.

Her heart almost stopped.

The woman inside the mirror quietly raised one finger.

She pointed behind Ada.

Ada turned around immediately.

Nobody was there.

When she looked back at the mirror...

The reflection was smiling again.

Just like nothing had happened.

Ada slowly stepped backward.

For the first time in her life...

She was afraid of her own face.

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌫 Chapter Two – Someone Else Was Living Her Life

Ada tried to forget what happened in the mirror.

She told herself she was only tired.

Maybe she needed more sleep.

Maybe she had been working too much.

That was the only thing that made sense.

She picked up her bag and left for work.

The city was already busy.

Cars were blowing their horns.

People rushed from one place to another.

Everything looked normal.

But Ada did not feel normal.

She kept thinking about the woman in the mirror.

At the fashion shop, her friend Tolu smiled as soon as she saw her.

"Ada, you left so early yesterday."

Ada looked confused.

"What do you mean?"

Tolu laughed.

"You came back after work."

"You said you forgot your phone."

Ada stopped sewing.

"I never came back."

Tolu frowned.

"Don't joke like that."

"You even stood right here."

"You smiled at me."

"You picked up your phone and left."

Ada slowly shook her head.

"My phone was with me all night."

The smile disappeared from Tolu's face.

She stared at Ada for several seconds.

"You are serious?"

Ada nodded.

A cold feeling settled inside both of them.

Neither of them spoke again.

Later that afternoon, Ada went to buy food during her break.

The woman selling rice smiled warmly.

"My daughter, you're back again."

Ada blinked.

"I haven't been here today."

The woman laughed.

"You young people like jokes."

"You bought food here less than one hour ago."

Ada's hands became cold.

"I didn't."

The woman slowly stopped smiling.

"You even wore those same clothes."

Ada quietly stepped away from the stall.

She was no longer hungry.

Something was terribly wrong.

That evening she rushed home.

As soon as she entered the house, her younger brother looked at her strangely.

"I thought you already came home."

Ada dropped her bag.

"What?"

"You came in."

"You went into your room."

"I even called your name."

"But you ignored me."

Ada slowly walked to her bedroom.

The door was still locked.

Exactly the way she left it that morning.

She opened it carefully.

Nothing had moved.

Everything was in its place.

She sat on the bed.

Her heart was beating fast.

People were seeing her...

Even when she wasn't there.

Late that night, rain began to fall.

Ada could not sleep.

She kept thinking about everything everyone had said.

Then...

She heard soft footsteps outside her room.

Slow...

Steady...

Almost like someone was walking through the house.

She quietly stood up.

She opened her bedroom door.

The hallway was empty.

She looked toward the sitting room.

Nobody.

Then she looked toward the mirror hanging at the end of the hallway.

A woman stood inside it.

She looked exactly like Ada.

But this time...

She slowly smiled.

Then she whispered,

"Everyone already believes I'm you."

🌙 End of Chapter Two
