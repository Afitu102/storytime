👻 The Ghost That Wore Her Face

🌿 African Folktale • 🕒 26 min read

👻 Chapter One – The Woman in the Glass

Lagos was never truly quiet.

Cars filled the roads every morning.

People hurried to work.

Street traders called out to customers.

Music floated from buses and small shops.

The city never seemed to sleep.

Ada had lived there all her life.

She worked in a small fashion shop where she sewed clothes for people.

Everyone liked Ada.

She smiled often.

She greeted everyone.

She never liked trouble.

Every evening after work, she walked home through the same streets.

It was quicker than waiting for a bus.

One rainy evening, the whole city looked different.

The roads were wet.

Street lights reflected on the water like tiny stars.

Most people had already gone home.

Ada opened her umbrella and continued walking.

As she passed an old building, she noticed something strange.

A broken shop window stood beside the road.

She looked into the glass without thinking.

She saw herself.

Or...

She thought she did.

The woman inside the glass looked exactly like her.

Same face.

Same clothes.

Same hairstyle.

But something was wrong.

The woman was smiling.

Ada wasn't.

Her heart skipped.

She quickly stepped away from the window.

When she looked again...

The reflection was normal.

She laughed softly.

"I must be tired."

She continued walking.

When she reached home, her younger brother was watching television.

Her mother was already serving dinner.

Everything felt normal again.

She forgot about the strange reflection.

That night...

Ada woke up suddenly.

Someone had whispered her name.

"Ada..."

She quickly sat up.

The room was dark.

Everyone else was asleep.

She listened carefully.

Nothing.

She shook her head.

"It was only a dream."

She lay down again.

The next morning she stood before the mirror while getting ready for work.

She adjusted her hair.

She smiled.

The mirror smiled back.

Then...

The reflection slowly stopped smiling.

Ada was still smiling.

Her heart almost stopped.

The woman inside the mirror quietly raised one finger.

She pointed behind Ada.

Ada turned around immediately.

Nobody was there.

When she looked back at the mirror...

The reflection was smiling again.

Just like nothing had happened.

Ada slowly stepped backward.

For the first time in her life...

She was afraid of her own face.
