🪘 The Cursed Drum of Kafira Village

🌿 African Folktale • 🕒 28 min read

🪘 Chapter One – The Drum Nobody Must Touch

Long ago, there was a small place called Kafira Village.

People there worked hard. They farmed. They fished. They laughed together every evening.

But there was one place nobody went.

It was an old hut at the edge of the village.

Inside that hut sat one very old drum.

The drum was bigger than a man.

It was covered with old skins, strange ropes, and little white shells.

Nobody knew who made it.

Nobody knew how old it was.

The old people only said one thing.

"Never touch that drum."

Children heard the warning from the day they could walk.

Even grown men stayed far away.

When the wind blew at night...

People said they could hear...

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

Nobody was beating it.

Still...

The sound came.

One old woman whispered,

"The drum is breathing again."

Another old man shook his head.

"No..."

"It is calling."

Nobody asked what it was calling.

Because everyone already knew.

It was calling for the next person.

Every year, one person disappeared.

No body.

No footprints.

Nothing.

Only the drum sound.

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

People stopped talking whenever they heard it.

Doors were locked.

Fires were put out.

Even dogs hid under beds.

Nobody wanted the drum to know they were awake.

One evening, a young hunter came back to the village.

His name was Tayo.

He was brave.

He feared nothing.

When the old people warned him about the drum...

He laughed.

"A drum cannot kill anybody."

The three old women looked at each other.

One of them slowly stood up.

She looked straight into Tayo's eyes.

Then she said,

"The last man who said those same words..."

"...never came back."

Tayo smiled anyway.

He picked up his spear.

"I will go and see this drum tomorrow."

The whole village became silent.

Even the birds stopped singing.

Far away...

From inside the old hut...

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

The drum had just heard his promise.

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌫 Chapter Two – The Beating in the Dark

The next morning came.

Nobody saw Tayo.

His mother knocked on his door.

No answer.

She pushed the door open.

His bed was empty.

His spear was gone.

His sandals were gone too.

The whole village knew where he had gone.

Nobody said it.

But everybody knew.

The old women were already sitting under the big tree.

Their eyes were closed.

One of them spoke.

"He has gone to wake it."

The other two nodded slowly.

Far away...

Tayo climbed the narrow path to the old hut.

The air felt heavy.

Even the trees looked dead.

No birds sang.

No insects made a sound.

He laughed to himself.

"They scared themselves for nothing."

Soon...

He saw the hut.

It looked older than before.

The roof was falling apart.

The door was hanging on one rope.

Tayo walked closer.

His smile slowly disappeared.

The drum was there.

Just as the old people had said.

It was huge.

Its black skin looked fresh.

Like somebody had just made it.

The little white shells around it moved.

But...

There was no wind.

Tayo frowned.

He reached out his hand.

Just before his fingers touched the drum...

Boom...

The sound came again.

Very loud.

Tayo quickly pulled back.

He looked around.

Nobody.

The hut was empty.

He laughed again.

"Who's trying to scare me?"

No answer.

So...

He hit the drum with his hand.

Boom!!

The ground shook.

The trees bent.

Birds flew into the sky.

Back in Kafira Village...

Everyone froze.

The old women slowly opened their eyes.

One of them whispered,

"He has done it."

Then...

A deep voice came from inside the hut.

It was not loud.

But it sounded close.

"Tayo..."

He turned quickly.

Nobody.

The voice came again.

"Tayo..."

This time...

It sounded like it was coming...

From inside the drum.

🌙 End of Chapter Two

🌳 Chapter Three – The Voice Inside the Drum

Tayo slowly stepped back.

His heart was beating fast.

He looked around again.

Nobody.

The hut was still empty.

Only the old drum stood before him.

Then...

Boom...

The drum beat by itself.

Tayo did not touch it.

Still...

It beat again.

Boom...

Boom...

The voice returned.

"Tayo..."

This time it sounded sad.

Like someone who had been waiting for many years.

Tayo swallowed hard.

"Who's there?"

The hut became quiet.

Then the drum answered.

"I have waited for you."

Tayo's eyes grew wide.

His legs wanted to run.

But they refused to move.

The shells hanging on the drum began to shake.

One by one...

They started falling to the ground.

Click...

Click...

Click...

The ropes around the drum slowly untied themselves.

The black skin stretched tighter.

It looked alive.

Suddenly...

A dark hand pushed out from the middle of the drum.

Tayo jumped back.

Then another hand appeared.

Then a face.

It was covered with black ash.

Its eyes were white.

It smiled.

A smile that made Tayo's whole body shake.

"Thank you..."

"You woke me."

Tayo grabbed his spear.

He pointed it at the strange figure.

"Stay back!"

The figure laughed.

Its laugh echoed across the forest.

The trees shook.

The sky became dark.

Back in Kafira Village...

People looked toward the forest.

They could hear the laugh.

Children started crying.

The three old women stood together.

One of them closed her eyes.

"It has left the drum."

Another whispered,

"Now it will come for the village."

Inside the hut...

The dark figure slowly stepped out of the drum.

It looked straight at Tayo.

Then it smiled again.

"You are the first..."

"But you will not be the last."

Suddenly...

It disappeared.

Just like smoke.

Tayo stood there alone.

The drum was silent again.

But outside...

Something was walking toward Kafira Village.

🌙 End of Chapter Three

🔥 Chapter Four – The First Night of Fear

Tayo ran as fast as he could.

He did not stop.

He did not look back.

Branches hit his face.

Sharp stones cut his feet.

Still...

He kept running.

When he finally reached Kafira Village...

Everybody was waiting.

Nobody asked where he had been.

They already knew.

The three old women walked toward him.

One looked into his eyes.

Another touched his forehead.

The last one whispered,

"You saw it."

Tayo nodded slowly.

His lips were shaking.

"It came out of the drum."

"It spoke to me."

The old women closed their eyes.

Nobody said another word.

The village chief stepped forward.

His face was full of fear.

"Lock every door."

"Nobody goes outside tonight."

Before the sun disappeared...

Every house was closed.

Even the cooking fires were put out.

The whole village became dark.

Children stayed close to their mothers.

Nobody laughed.

Nobody talked loudly.

Then...

Night came.

For a long time...

Nothing happened.

Some people started thinking...

Maybe it was over.

Then...

Boom...

The drum beat again.

But this time...

It was not coming from the forest.

It was much closer.

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

The sound moved through the village.

Like somebody was carrying the drum.

A little girl looked through a small hole in her window.

She gasped.

"Mama..."

"Someone is outside."

Her mother quickly covered her mouth.

"Don't look."

"Whatever you hear..."

"Don't open the door."

Outside...

Slow footsteps echoed.

Step...

Step...

Step...

Then...

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

Someone knocked on Tayo's door.

He held his breath.

Nobody in the house moved.

Then a voice came.

It sounded exactly like his late father.

"Tayo..."

"My son..."

"Open the door."

Tayo's eyes filled with tears.

His father had died five years ago.

But the voice...

It sounded real.

Very real.

He slowly stood up.

He began walking toward the door.

His hand reached for the wooden lock.

Just as he was about to open it...

One of the old women screamed from outside the house,

"Tayo!!"

"That is NOT your father!"

🌙 End of Chapter Four
