🪘 The Cursed Drum of Kafira Village

🌿 African Folktale • 🕒 28 min read

🪘 Chapter One – The Drum Nobody Must Touch

Long ago, there was a small place called Kafira Village.

People there worked hard. They farmed. They fished. They laughed together every evening.

But there was one place nobody went.

It was an old hut at the edge of the village.

Inside that hut sat one very old drum.

The drum was bigger than a man.

It was covered with old skins, strange ropes, and little white shells.

Nobody knew who made it.

Nobody knew how old it was.

The old people only said one thing.

"Never touch that drum."

Children heard the warning from the day they could walk.

Even grown men stayed far away.

When the wind blew at night...

People said they could hear...

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

Nobody was beating it.

Still...

The sound came.

One old woman whispered,

"The drum is breathing again."

Another old man shook his head.

"No..."

"It is calling."

Nobody asked what it was calling.

Because everyone already knew.

It was calling for the next person.

Every year, one person disappeared.

No body.

No footprints.

Nothing.

Only the drum sound.

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

People stopped talking whenever they heard it.

Doors were locked.

Fires were put out.

Even dogs hid under beds.

Nobody wanted the drum to know they were awake.

One evening, a young hunter came back to the village.

His name was Tayo.

He was brave.

He feared nothing.

When the old people warned him about the drum...

He laughed.

"A drum cannot kill anybody."

The three old women looked at each other.

One of them slowly stood up.

She looked straight into Tayo's eyes.

Then she said,

"The last man who said those same words..."

"...never came back."

Tayo smiled anyway.

He picked up his spear.

"I will go and see this drum tomorrow."

The whole village became silent.

Even the birds stopped singing.

Far away...

From inside the old hut...

Boom...

Boom...

Boom...

The drum had just heard his promise.
