👻 The Witch in Babylon

📚 Classic Authors • 🕒 50 min read

🕯️ Chapter One – The Child in the Dream

Babylon was asleep.

The streets were empty.

The palace gates were closed.

Only the guards walked through the dark.

Far from the palace stood an old house.

Nobody went near it.

People called it the House of Ashes.

That was where the witch lived.

Her name was Naqira.

She was feared by everyone.

Naqira did not care about the king.

She did not fear the soldiers.

She feared nobody.

People said she could hear a person's name from miles away.

People said she could make a man forget his own family.

Some said she could enter a person's dream.

But the worst thing was this.

People who angered Naqira disappeared.

Nobody found their bodies.

Nobody asked questions.

Those who asked questions disappeared too.

That night, a little girl named Talia was sleeping beside her mother.

She was only nine years old.

She had never seen the witch.

She had never spoken her name.

But that night, Talia dreamed about her.

She was standing in a strange courtyard.

The ground was covered with black dust.

Tall walls surrounded her.

There was no door.

No window.

No way out.

Then she heard footsteps.

Slow footsteps.

Step.

Step.

Step.

Talia turned.

An old woman stood behind her.

Her hair was completely white.

Her eyes were dark.

She held a long wooden staff.

A small red stone hung from the top of it.

Talia knew who she was.

Naqira.

The witch smiled.

"You know my name."

Talia stepped back.

"Leave me alone."

Naqira laughed.

"This is my dream."

Talia looked around.

The black dust began to move.

It formed the shape of human hands.

The hands crawled across the ground.

Talia screamed.

Naqira raised her staff.

The hands stopped.

"Do you know how many people I have buried?"

Talia shook her head.

Naqira smiled.

"Too many to count."

She walked closer.

"Kings have begged me."

"Soldiers have run from me."

"Strong men have cried before me."

She stopped in front of Talia.

"But you are only a child."

Talia looked at her.

"Then why did you bring me here?"

Naqira's smile disappeared.

"I didn't."

The wind suddenly became loud.

Naqira looked behind Talia.

Her face changed.

For the first time, the witch looked afraid.

"Who sent you?"

Talia did not know.

"I don't know."

Naqira stepped backward.

"Wake up."

Talia frowned.

"What?"

"Wake up now!"

The courtyard began to shake.

The walls cracked.

The black dust rose into the air.

A voice came from somewhere above them.

"Talia."

She looked up.

"Who are you?"

The voice answered.

"I am the one she could not kill."

Naqira screamed.

"No!"

A bright light appeared behind the witch.

Naqira dropped her staff.

She turned to run.

The light followed her.

Then the voice spoke again.

"Talia, listen carefully."

Talia stood still.

"She must die."

Talia's eyes widened.

Naqira looked at her.

"Do not listen!"

The voice continued.

"She will kill many more."

"She will come for your family."

"Only you can stop her."

Talia began to cry.

"I am only a child."

The voice became softer.

"That is why she will never suspect you."

Naqira rushed toward Talia.

Talia screamed.

The entire courtyard went dark.

Then Talia woke up.

She sat up quickly.

Her mother was sleeping beside her.

Talia touched her face.

She was sweating.

Her heart was beating fast.

She looked around the room.

Everything was normal.

Or so she thought.

A strange smell filled the room.

Smoke.

Talia got out of bed.

She walked toward the window.

Someone was standing outside.

An old woman.

White hair.

Dark eyes.

A long wooden staff.

Talia froze.

The woman smiled.

Then she disappeared.

Talia ran to her mother.

"Mother!"

Her mother woke up.

"What happened?"

Talia pointed at the window.

"The witch was outside."

Her mother became pale.

"What witch?"

"Naqira."

Her mother sat up.

"Never say that name again."

Talia stared at her.

"Why?"

Her mother stood quickly.

She locked the door.

Then she locked the window.

"Because she hears it."

Talia whispered:

"She came to me in my dream."

Her mother stopped.

"What did she say?"

Talia looked at her.

"She said someone sent me."

Her mother said nothing.

"She was afraid."

Her mother's hands began to shake.

"What else?"

Talia took a breath.

"A voice told me to kill her."

Her mother dropped the lamp.

It hit the floor.

The flame went out.

The room became dark.

Then someone knocked on their door.

Three times.

Knock.

Knock.

Knock.

Talia's mother grabbed her tightly.

A voice came from outside.

"Open the door."

Talia knew the voice.

It was Naqira.

Her mother covered Talia's mouth.

The voice came again.

"I know what the child saw."

The door began to shake.

Talia's mother whispered into her ear.

"Whatever happens, do not let her touch you."

The door shook harder.

Then it broke open.

Naqira stood in the doorway.

Her eyes were glowing.

She looked directly at Talia.

And smiled.

"So the dream has chosen you."

Talia stepped backward.

Naqira raised her hand.

The room suddenly became freezing cold.

Then Talia saw something in the witch's hand.

A small black knife.

Naqira whispered:

"Before sunrise, one of us will be dead."

Talia looked at her mother.

Then she remembered the voice from the dream.

"Only you can stop her."

Talia slowly reached beneath her pillow.

Her fingers touched something cold.

It was not there before she went to sleep.

A small silver blade.

And carved into its handle were three words.

DO NOT MISS.

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌑 Chapter Two – The Silver Blade

Talia stared at the small silver blade.

Her hands were shaking.

Naqira stood near the broken door.

Her black knife was still in her hand.

"Give it to me."

Talia held the blade tighter.

"No."

Naqira smiled.

"You don't even know how to use it."

Talia's mother stepped between them.

"Leave my daughter alone."

Naqira lifted one finger.

Talia's mother suddenly fell against the wall.

Talia screamed.

"Mother!"

She ran toward her.

Naqira moved faster.

She grabbed Talia's arm.

Talia felt a sharp pain.

The silver blade fell from her hand.

Naqira looked down.

Before she could pick it up, Talia kicked her.

Naqira stumbled backward.

Talia grabbed the blade.

She ran toward her mother.

Naqira screamed.

"Come back!"

Talia did not stop.

She pulled her mother toward the back room.

Naqira raised her hand.

The wooden table flew across the room.

It crashed into the wall.

Talia pushed her mother through the doorway.

Then she slammed the door.

Something hit it from the other side.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

The door began to crack.

Her mother held her chest.

"Talia, listen to me."

Talia knelt beside her.

"What?"

"That blade is the only thing that can hurt her."

Talia looked at it.

"Who gave it to me?"

Her mother looked frightened.

"I don't know."

A loud crash came from the other room.

Naqira had broken through the door.

Talia's mother grabbed her hand.

"Run to the old temple."

Talia shook her head.

"I won't leave you."

"You must."

Her mother pushed a small pouch into her hand.

"Take this."

Talia opened it.

Inside was a small bronze key.

She looked confused.

"What does it open?"

Her mother whispered:

"A room beneath the temple."

Talia stared at her.

"Why?"

Her mother looked toward the door.

"Because that is where your father hid the truth."

Talia froze.

She had never known her father.

"My father?"

Her mother nodded.

"He knew Naqira before anyone knew her name."

The door burst open.

Naqira entered.

Her face was filled with anger.

"You should have told her years ago."

Talia's mother stood.

"She was a child."

"She is still a child."

Naqira raised her knife.

Talia moved in front of her mother.

Naqira laughed.

"You think that little blade can save you?"

Talia said nothing.

Naqira rushed forward.

Talia raised the silver blade.

Naqira suddenly stopped.

Her eyes widened.

The blade was glowing.

A thin white light moved across its edge.

Naqira stepped back.

"Where did you get that?"

Talia looked at the blade.

"It was under my pillow."

Naqira's face changed.

"No."

The walls began to shake.

A deep sound came from outside.

Naqira looked toward the broken window.

For the first time, she looked worried.

Talia noticed.

"You're afraid."

Naqira looked at her.

"You don't know what you are holding."

Talia raised the blade.

"Then tell me."

Naqira said nothing.

Talia stepped closer.

"Tell me what happened to my father."

Naqira's eyes became cold.

"Your father made a foolish choice."

"What choice?"

"He tried to kill me."

Talia looked at her mother.

"Did he fail?"

Naqira smiled.

"Yes."

Talia's mother whispered:

"Don't believe her."

Naqira turned toward her.

"You should have died with him."

She lifted her hand.

Talia moved without thinking.

She swung the silver blade.

The blade touched Naqira's arm.

Naqira screamed.

Dark smoke burst from the wound.

She fell to her knees.

Talia stared at the blade.

There was no blood.

Only black smoke.

Naqira looked at the wound.

"You little fool."

Her voice had changed.

It sounded deeper.

Older.

Talia stepped back.

Naqira suddenly grabbed her mother's neck.

"Drop the blade."

Talia froze.

Her mother struggled.

"Don't."

Talia lowered the blade.

Naqira smiled.

"Good girl."

Then someone crashed through the roof.

A young man landed between them.

He carried a long spear.

Naqira released Talia's mother.

The young man attacked.

Naqira blocked the spear with her staff.

The room shook.

Talia pulled her mother away.

"Who is he?"

Her mother looked at the young man.

"His name is Dagan."

Dagan struck Naqira across the chest.

She flew backward.

She crashed through the wall.

Talia stared in shock.

Dagan turned toward them.

"Take the girl and leave."

Talia's mother shook her head.

"We cannot."

"Why?"

She pointed at Talia's hand.

"She has the blade."

Dagan looked at it.

His face became serious.

"Then we have very little time."

A strange laugh came from the broken wall.

Naqira stood again.

Her wound was closing.

Dagan's eyes widened.

"That is impossible."

Naqira smiled.

"You should have learned that before coming here."

She raised both hands.

Every lamp in the house exploded.

The room went dark.

Talia heard screaming.

She heard Dagan fighting.

She heard her mother calling her name.

Then silence.

Talia could not see anyone.

She held the silver blade tightly.

A hand touched her shoulder.

She almost screamed.

A voice whispered:

"Do not move."

It was Dagan.

Talia whispered:

"Where is my mother?"

Dagan did not answer.

Talia's heart began to race.

"Where is she?"

Dagan finally spoke.

"Naqira took her."

Talia's eyes filled with tears.

"Where?"

Dagan looked toward the broken wall.

"To the palace."

Talia gripped the silver blade.

"Then we go after her."

Dagan shook his head.

"You don't understand."

Talia looked at him.

"What?"

Dagan lowered his voice.

"Naqira did not take your mother to kill her."

Talia waited.

"She took her to use her."

Talia's face became pale.

"For what?"

Dagan looked at the silver blade.

"To bring your father back."

Talia stopped breathing.

Her father had been dead for years.

At least, that was what her mother had told her.

Then a loud bell rang from the direction of the palace.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

Dagan looked toward the sound.

"She has started."

Talia held the blade close.

"Then let's stop her."

Dagan looked at the little girl.

For a moment, he said nothing.

Then he nodded.

"Tonight, Babylon will learn the truth."

🌙 End of Chapter Two
