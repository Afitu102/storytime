Chapter 3 — The Truth She Never Knew

Amara stared at Daniel, her heart beating faster than it ever had before.

“Who is that man?” she whispered.

Daniel looked toward the trees where the stranger had disappeared.

“I have seen him before,” Daniel said.

Amara looked at him in surprise. “You know him?”

“Not well enough to trust him.”

Amara folded her mother's letter carefully, but the questions inside her mind refused to disappear.

“Daniel, tell me everything about my father.”

Daniel took a deep breath before answering.

“Your father came from a powerful family. They had money, influence, and enemies. They would never have accepted your mother.”

Amara frowned. “Why?”

“Because they believed your mother was beneath them.”

Daniel paused for a moment. “But your father loved her anyway.”

Something inside Amara changed.

All her life, she had believed that her father simply abandoned them.

Now she wondered if everything she had been told was a lie.

“What happened to him?” she asked.

Daniel lowered his voice. “Your father discovered that someone close to his family wanted him dead.”

Amara's eyes widened. “Dead?”

“Yes.”

“Who?”

Daniel hesitated before answering.

“His older brother.”

Amara could hardly believe what she had heard.

“His own brother wanted him dead?”

Daniel nodded. “It was because of the family inheritance. Your father was expected to control everything one day, and his brother could not accept that.”

“So he ran away?”

“He disappeared because it was the only way to stay alive.”

Amara remembered how her mother had always avoided talking about her father.

Whenever Amara asked questions about him, her mother would quickly change the subject.

Now she finally understood why.

Daniel reached into his pocket and brought out another folded piece of paper.

“Your mother left this for you.”

Amara stared at it. “Another letter?”

“Yes.”

Amara took the letter with trembling hands and slowly opened it.

The moment she saw the handwriting, tears filled her eyes.

She recognized her mother's handwriting immediately.

“My dearest Amara, if you are reading this, then the truth has finally begun to find you.”

“There are things I could never tell you while I was alive because knowing them would have placed you in danger.”

“Do not trust everyone who claims to know your father.”

Amara read the words slowly, her hands becoming colder with every sentence.

Then she reached the final warning.

“If Daniel tells you that your father is waiting for you, don't go to him.”

Amara's hands suddenly stopped shaking.

She slowly looked up at Daniel.

“Why would my mother say this about you?”

Daniel's face became unreadable.

“Because she knew what I was forced to become.”

Amara stared at him. “What does that mean?”

Before Daniel could answer, a voice came from behind them.

“Ask him who started the fire.”

Amara quickly turned around.

The mysterious man stood several steps away, watching Daniel carefully.

“I knew your mother,” the man said to Amara.

“I was there the night everything changed.”

Daniel stepped forward. “Leave her alone.”

The man ignored him.

“Your mother did not die because of an accident.”

Amara's heart sank.

“What are you saying?”

The man looked directly at Daniel.

“Ask Daniel who started the fire.”

Daniel's expression hardened.

“It was your father's brother.”

Silence fell around them.

Amara looked at Daniel in disbelief.

“Your father's brother wanted him dead,” Daniel continued. “But when he discovered that your mother had taken you away, he realized that you could eventually lead him back to your father.”

Amara's voice trembled. “Me?”

Daniel nodded.

The mysterious man stepped closer.

“Your father's brother knows you are alive.”

Amara's face became pale.

Daniel grabbed her hand.

“We have to find your father before they do.”

Amara pulled her hand away.

“No.”

Daniel looked at her. “What?”

Amara held up her mother's letter.

“My mother warned me about you.”

Daniel's face changed.

“Amara...”

“She told me not to go anywhere with you if you said my father was waiting for me.”

Daniel lowered his eyes.

For several seconds, neither of them spoke.

Then suddenly—

“BANG!”

A gunshot echoed through the area.

Amara screamed.

Daniel grabbed her hand.

“Run!”

They raced toward the village as another gunshot echoed behind them.

Amara's heart pounded against her chest.

One thought kept repeating in her mind.

Someone knew she was alive.

Someone was hunting for her.

When they finally reached Amara's house, Daniel suddenly stopped.

Amara looked at him. “Why did you stop?”

Daniel stared at the door.

The door was slightly open.

Amara's eyes widened.

“I locked that door before I left.”

Daniel immediately moved in front of her.

“Stay behind me.”

They slowly stepped closer to the house.

Someone was already inside.

Whoever it was had been waiting for Amara to return.
