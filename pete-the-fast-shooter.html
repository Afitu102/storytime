🔫 Pete the Fast Shooter

🌍 African Folktales • 🕒 30 min read

🏹 Chapter One – Pete the Fast Shooter

In the village of Umuoma, there was one hunter whose name was always mentioned whenever the people talked about hunting.

His name was Pete.

Pete was not the biggest hunter in the village. He was not the strongest either. He did not carry the biggest bow, and he never tried to make people afraid of him.

But there was one thing Pete had that made other hunters respect him.

He was incredibly fast.

When Pete raised his bow, there was hardly enough time for another person to notice what he was doing before his arrow was already flying through the air.

The other hunters had seen it happen many times, but they still could not understand it.

One morning, Pete went into the forest with several other hunters. The grass was tall, the trees were thick, and the sound of birds could be heard from every direction.

The hunters walked quietly, looking carefully from one side to another.

Everyone wanted to be the first to find an animal.

After walking for some time, one of the hunters suddenly stopped.

“Wait,” he whispered.

Everyone became still.

Something was moving among the bushes.

The hunters slowly turned their eyes toward the sound.

There it was.

An animal had stepped out from behind the bushes and was moving across the open grass.

One of the hunters immediately reached for his bow.

Another hunter also prepared himself.

They wanted to make the first move.

But before either of them could properly aim, Pete had already raised his bow.

He pulled the string back.

His eyes stayed fixed on the animal.

For a brief moment, everything seemed to become quiet.

Then Pete released the arrow.

Whoosh!

The arrow flew straight toward the animal.

The animal dropped.

The other hunters stood there in disbelief.

One of them was still holding his arrow in his hand.

Another had not even finished pulling his bowstring.

“Pete!” one of them shouted. “How did you do that?”

Pete simply smiled.

“What did I do?”

“You already got it!”

Pete looked at the animal and then back at his friends.

“It was there. I saw it. So I took my chance.”

The hunters shook their heads.

That was Pete.

He never wasted time when an opportunity came.

The strange thing was that Pete did not hunt because he wanted people to praise him.

He genuinely loved hunting.

Whenever he held his bow and walked into the forest, he felt happy.

He loved the quietness of the forest. He loved watching the movement of animals. He loved the feeling of returning home after a successful hunt.

Most of all, Pete never went into the forest with hatred or greed in his heart.

He always believed that he would return with something useful.

Before leaving his house, he would prepare himself and speak positive words.

“As I go out, I will win. I will come back with meat. I will come back with something worth it.”

Those were the words Pete repeated to himself.

He said them with confidence.

He did not say them because he thought words alone would bring food to his house. Pete still worked hard. He watched carefully, practiced his skills, respected the forest, and remained patient.

But he believed that a good mind could help a person face a difficult day with courage.

And somehow, whenever Pete went hunting, his luck seemed to follow him.

The other hunters noticed it too.

If five hunters entered the forest together, Pete was often the one who returned first with something.

If ten hunters went looking for animals, Pete somehow found the right path.

Sometimes another hunter would spot an animal first and proudly prepare to shoot.

Before he could release his arrow, Pete would already have taken his shot.

It became a common joke in the village.

“Never compete with Pete in the forest,” the hunters would say.

“You will only return tired.”

Pete would laugh whenever he heard them.

He never insulted the other hunters.

He never told them that he was better than them.

That was another thing people admired about him.

Pete was a man with a good heart and a clear mind.

If another hunter returned home without catching anything, Pete did not laugh at him.

If someone asked him for help, he helped.

If a family had no meat, Pete was willing to share what he had.

Because of this, people respected him even more.

His speed made people talk about him, but his character made people love him.

One evening, after another successful hunting trip, Pete returned to the village carrying his catch.

As usual, people gathered around him.

“Pete is back!”

“Look at what he brought again!”

“Did anyone even see the animal before he shot it?”

Everyone laughed.

Pete smiled and continued walking.

But among the crowd were several young hunters who were not laughing.

They were watching him carefully.

They wanted to know his secret.

They had tried everything they knew.

They had practiced their aim.

They had changed their bows.

They had followed experienced hunters into the forest.

Still, nobody could match Pete.

One of the young hunters finally said what everyone else had been thinking.

“There must be something he knows that we don't know.”

The others agreed.

The question soon spread throughout the village.

What made Pete so successful?

Was it his eyes?

Was it his hands?

Was it his bow?

Was it his strength?

Or was there another secret behind his success?

The village elders also became curious.

They had watched Pete for years, and they knew that his success was no longer something people could simply ignore.

So one afternoon, the village elders sent for him.

When Pete arrived, he saw several elders sitting under the large tree in the village square.

Some hunters were also there.

Pete greeted everyone respectfully.

One of the elders smiled and asked him to sit down.

“Pete,” the elder began, “we have called you here because there is something we want from you.”

Pete looked around.

“What is it?”

“We want you to teach the other hunters.”

Pete was surprised.

“Teach them what?”

The elder laughed.

“Your secret.”

The hunters around them smiled.

Everyone wanted to hear Pete's answer.

Pete looked at them quietly.

He knew they were expecting something big.

Perhaps they thought he had a special hunting technique.

Perhaps they thought he had discovered a secret path in the forest.

Perhaps they thought he had some special strength that nobody else possessed.

But Pete knew the truth.

His secret was much simpler than they imagined.

He stood up and looked at the hunters.

“You want to know my secret?”

“Yes!” they answered together.

Pete smiled.

Then he said something that left everyone silent.

“My secret is the way I prepare my mind before I leave home.”

The hunters looked at one another.

That was not the answer they expected.

Pete continued.

“Before I go out, I tell myself that I will succeed. I tell myself that I will return with something useful. I believe it, and then I go out and do my best.”

One of the hunters frowned.

“Is that all?”

Pete nodded.

“That is where I start.”

The elders looked at one another.

The hunters were still not convinced.

But Pete had not finished explaining.

He raised his hand and said,

“When I go out, I say, ‘As I go out, I will win. I will come back with meat. I will come back with something worth it.’ Then I go with a clear mind, watch carefully, and do my work.”

The hunters became quiet.

Nobody knew whether his words would work for them.

But the elders decided that there was only one way to find out.

They asked the hunters to try it for themselves.

The next morning, several hunters prepared to enter the forest.

Before they left, they remembered Pete's words.

Some laughed.

Some felt embarrassed.

Others decided to take it seriously.

But none of them knew that before the sun went down, something was going to happen that would make the whole village talk about Pete's advice.

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌿 Chapter Two – The Day the Other Hunters Tried

The next morning, the village woke up earlier than usual.

The hunters were already preparing for the forest.

They had heard Pete's words the previous evening, and many of them were determined to try what he had taught them.

Some were excited.

Some were doubtful.

And a few were quietly laughing because they could not believe that speaking positive words could make any difference.

One of the older hunters shook his head as he checked his bow.

“I have hunted for more than twenty years,” he said. “I have never heard that talking to yourself will make an animal come closer.”

The younger hunters laughed.

“But Pete said we should have a clear mind,” another hunter replied.

“Then let us have a clear mind and see what happens.”

Everyone laughed again.

Not far away, Pete was getting ready to leave.

He checked his equipment, looked at the morning sky, and took a deep breath.

Before stepping out of his compound, he said his usual words.

“As I go out, I will win. I will come back with meat. I will come back with something worth it.”

He said it calmly.

Then he started walking.

Several hunters followed behind him.

They wanted to see whether Pete would do anything differently.

When they reached the edge of the forest, Pete stopped.

He looked at the trees and listened carefully.

The others also stopped.

For a few seconds, nobody spoke.

Then one of the young hunters whispered,

“Pete, what are you looking for?”

Pete smiled.

“Nothing yet.”

“Then why did you stop?”

“I am listening.”

The hunter became quiet.

Pete had always been like that.

He did not rush into the forest simply because he wanted to catch something.

He watched.

He listened.

He allowed his mind to settle.

The other hunters began to understand that Pete's confidence was not the only thing that made him good at hunting.

He paid attention to everything around him.

The movement of the grass.

The sound of birds.

The direction of the wind.

Even the smallest sound could make him stop and look.

The hunters continued deeper into the forest.

After some time, one of them noticed movement in the distance.

“There!”

Everyone turned.

An animal was moving through the tall grass.

Immediately, the hunters reached for their bows.

This time, they were determined to beat Pete.

One hunter moved quickly to get a better position.

Another raised his bow.

A third hunter whispered,

“I saw it first.”

Nobody wanted to wait.

But Pete remained still.

He watched the animal carefully.

He did not panic.

He did not allow the excitement to control him.

Then the animal moved again.

Pete raised his bow.

One of the hunters noticed him and whispered,

“Pete is going to shoot.”

Before the other hunter could release his own arrow, Pete's arrow had already left the bow.

It flew quickly and found its mark.

The hunters froze.

Again.

Pete had done it again.

One of the hunters lowered his bow slowly.

“Honestly, I don't understand this man.”

Another hunter laughed.

“We have been standing here preparing to shoot, and Pete has already finished.”

Pete walked toward the animal.

He picked it up and returned to the group.

Nobody spoke for a moment.

Then the oldest hunter finally asked,

“Pete, tell us the truth. What kind of strength do you have?”

Pete laughed.

“Strength?”

“Yes. There is something different about you.”

Pete shook his head.

“I am not stronger than you.”

The hunter looked at him suspiciously.

“Then why does this keep happening?”

Pete placed the animal down.

“Because I don't enter the forest already thinking that I will fail.”

The hunters became quiet.

Pete continued.

“When I leave my home, I believe I will return with something useful. Then I stay focused on what I am doing.”

One of the young hunters nodded slowly.

“So the words are not magic?”

Pete smiled.

“No. Words do not replace hard work. But the way you speak to yourself can change the way you face your work.”

The hunters listened carefully.

Pete looked around at them.

“If you tell yourself that you cannot succeed before you even begin, you may give up too quickly. But if you tell yourself that you can do it, you give yourself a reason to keep trying.”

The older hunter nodded.

That explanation made more sense to him.

But the young hunters were still curious.

They wanted to experience it themselves.

So they continued hunting.

Later that day, something surprising happened.

One of the youngest hunters found himself alone near a small clearing.

He remembered Pete's words.

“As I go out, I will win. I will come back with meat. I will come back with something worth it.”

He repeated the words quietly.

Then he took a deep breath.

For the first time that morning, he stopped worrying about whether he would succeed.

He simply focused.

A few moments later, he heard movement in the grass.

His heart began beating quickly.

He almost became frightened.

But then he remembered to stay calm.

He watched carefully.

When the right moment came, he took his shot.

The animal ran a short distance before stopping.

The young hunter could hardly believe what had happened.

He had succeeded.

He stood there smiling.

Then he began laughing with excitement.

When he returned to the others, he proudly showed them what he had brought back.

The hunters gathered around him.

“You got one!”

“I actually got one!” he replied.

Everyone laughed.

Even Pete was happy for him.

Pete placed his hand on the young hunter's shoulder.

“You see? Keep your mind clear. Keep learning. Keep practicing.”

The young hunter nodded.

That evening, the hunters returned to the village with their catches.

For the first time, several of them had experienced something they had never expected.

They had not suddenly become better hunters than Pete.

They had simply stopped allowing fear and doubt to control them.

The villagers gathered around them as they came home.

Everyone wanted to hear what had happened in the forest.

One of the hunters pointed at Pete.

“Ask him!”

Pete laughed.

“Why me?”

“Because everything started with you.”

The village elders smiled.

They were beginning to understand why Pete had been successful for so long.

It was not simply about how quickly he could use his bow.

It was about the confidence he carried before he even entered the forest.

Still, one question remained.

Could Pete's simple words really help the whole village?

The elders decided they wanted to find out.

And they were about to ask Pete to do something he had never done before.

They wanted him to teach every hunter in the village.

But nobody knew that the next hunting trip would test Pete himself.

For the first time, Pete would enter the forest with hunters watching his every move.

And this time, they would not only be watching his speed.

They would be watching to see whether his famous confidence would still stand when luck seemed to turn against him.
