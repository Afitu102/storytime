🐈 Chapter One – The Cat That Came Every Night

Everybody in Ashwood Street knew the old white house.

Nobody wanted to go near it.

Not in the day.

And never at night.

People said the house had been empty for almost forty years.

The old owner died there.

After that...

No family stayed there for more than one week.

Some ran away.

Some disappeared.

Others refused to talk about what they saw inside.

The only thing everyone agreed on...

Was the black cat.

Every single night...

The same black cat walked out of the house.

It never ran.

It never meowed.

It only walked slowly down the road.

Looking straight ahead.

Like it knew exactly where it was going.

Nobody dared to stop it.

Nobody dared to follow it.

One cold evening...

A young writer named Daniel moved into the small house opposite the old building.

He did not believe ghost stories.

He laughed whenever people talked about haunted houses.

"Old stories are just stories," he always said.

The neighbours only smiled.

One old woman looked at him and quietly said,

"Just don't follow the black cat."

Daniel laughed.

"A cat?"

"You're all scared of a cat?"

The old woman walked away.

She did not answer.

That night...

Daniel sat by his window writing his next mystery book.

Rain gently hit the roof.

The street was quiet.

Then...

He heard footsteps.

Very soft.

Tap...

Tap...

Tap...

He looked outside.

There it was.

A black cat.

Its fur looked darker than the night.

Its eyes shined like small white lights.

But something else caught Daniel's attention.

The cat was carrying something in its mouth.

A white piece of lace.

It looked like part of a woman's dress.

The cat stopped in the middle of the road.

Slowly...

It turned its head.

It looked straight at Daniel.

Neither of them moved.

After a few seconds...

The cat slowly walked toward the old white house.

Daniel smiled.

"So that's the famous ghost cat."

He grabbed his flashlight.

He put on his jacket.

He quietly opened his front door.

Across the street...

The black cat was waiting.

It looked at him once.

Then it walked through the broken gate of the old house.

Daniel followed.

The gate closed behind him by itself.

BANG!

Daniel quickly turned around.

Nobody was there.

The street outside was empty.

The rain had suddenly stopped.

The whole place became strangely quiet.

Then...

From somewhere inside the house...

A woman's voice softly whispered,

"You finally came..."
