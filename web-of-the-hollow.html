🕷 Chapter One – The Thing Above the Trees

The forest had no birds that night.

No crickets.

No sound from the leaves.

Only the slow sound of footsteps.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Mara Vey walked along the old forest road.

She held a small iron lamp in one hand.

The flame inside it was weak.

Each time the wind passed, the flame bent low.

Mara stopped and looked behind her.

Nothing.

The road was empty.

Tall trees stood on both sides.

Their branches joined above her head.

They looked like long black fingers.

Mara pulled her dark cloak closer.

She had been walking for almost an hour.

The old map said the village was close.

Very close.

But the village had not appeared.

Not even a single light.

Not one house.

Mara reached into her coat and touched the small silver watch hanging from a chain.

11:47.

She frowned.

The watch had stopped moving three hours ago.

Yet the hands had suddenly started again when she entered the forest.

That was the first thing that frightened her.

The second was the sound.

A soft scratching sound came from somewhere above.

Mara slowly raised the lamp.

The light touched the branches.

Nothing.

She lowered it.

Scratch.

She looked up again.

Still nothing.

Then something fell from the tree.

It landed beside her.

Mara jumped back.

A dead crow lay on the road.

Its wings were spread.

There was no blood.

No wound.

Its eyes were open.

Mara stepped closer.

A thin white thread was wrapped around one of its legs.

She knew that thread.

Her grandfather had told her about it when she was a child.

Spider silk.

But not normal spider silk.

He had called it black silk.

He had also told her one rule.

"Never follow the silk."

Mara remembered laughing at him.

She was twelve then.

Her grandfather had looked at her with a serious face.

"If you ever see a strand tied to a tree, leave the forest."

She had asked why.

He had not answered.

He had only said one more thing.

"Some spiders build webs."

"Some spiders build doors."

Mara stared at the white strand beside the dead crow.

Her grandfather had been dead for eleven years.

Yet his warning suddenly felt very close.

She turned around.

The way back was gone.

Mara froze.

The road behind her had changed.

There was no road.

Only trees.

Thick trees.

Black trees.

She raised the lamp higher.

The forest looked endless.

Mara took one slow step backward.

A branch cracked behind her.

She spun around.

Something moved between the trees.

A tall shape.

It stood very still.

Mara lifted the lamp.

The light reached the shape.

A person stood there.

A long black cloak covered the body.

The hood hid the face.

Mara could not see the hands.

She held the lamp tighter.

"Hello?"

The figure did not move.

Mara took a step forward.

The figure took one step backward.

Mara stopped.

The figure stopped too.

She felt a cold drop slide down her neck.

She looked at the ground.

White silk covered the roots of the trees.

It stretched from one trunk to another.

Then she noticed something worse.

The silk was moving.

Not from the wind.

It was pulling itself toward the figure.

Mara raised her eyes.

The cloaked figure slowly lifted one arm.

It pointed above her.

Mara looked up.

Her breath stopped.

Something huge was hanging between the trees.

At first she thought it was a mass of branches.

Then it moved.

Eight long legs slowly unfolded from the darkness.

The creature was bigger than a horse.

Its body was covered in thick black hair.

Its legs stretched from tree to tree.

The huge spider lowered its body.

Its face appeared above Mara.

Two black eyes stared down.

Then two more opened.

Then two more.

Mara stumbled backward.

The lamp fell from her hand.

The flame went out.

Darkness swallowed the road.

The spider moved.

One leg struck the ground beside Mara.

She screamed and ran.

Branches hit her face.

Roots caught her boots.

She did not stop.

Behind her came the sound of many legs moving over leaves.

Fast.

Too fast.

Mara looked back.

The spider was coming.

It moved between the trees like it owned the forest.

Its legs struck the ground.

The earth shook with every step.

Mara ran harder.

She saw a fallen tree ahead.

She jumped over it.

Her boot slipped.

She fell hard.

The silver watch flew from her hand.

It landed in the leaves.

Mara reached for it.

The spider stopped.

Silence returned.

Mara slowly looked up.

The creature was standing only a few steps away.

Its front legs were raised.

It did not attack.

It only watched her.

Mara grabbed the watch.

The hands had started moving.

11:48.

One minute had passed.

The spider lowered its head.

A long strand of silk dropped from its mouth.

It touched the ground.

Mara stared at it.

The silk slowly formed letters in the dirt.

"YOU CAME BACK."

Her heart began beating faster.

She had never been here before.

At least she thought she had not.

The spider moved closer.

Mara closed her eyes.

She waited for the pain.

Nothing happened.

Instead, she heard a voice.

Not from the spider.

From behind her.

"Mara."

Her eyes opened.

The cloaked figure stood there.

Closer than before.

Mara turned toward it.

"Who are you?"

The figure slowly raised one hand.

A pale finger appeared beneath the sleeve.

It pointed toward the spider.

The giant creature lowered itself.

Its huge head came close to the ground.

Mara could see her own face reflected in its dark eyes.

Then the spider moved aside.

Behind it was a narrow opening between the trees.

Mara had not seen it before.

A path.

The cloaked figure walked toward the path.

It stopped.

Then it looked back at Mara.

Mara knew what it wanted.

Follow.

Every part of her told her not to.

But something else pulled her forward.

A memory.

Her grandfather standing beside a window.

His hand resting on the same silver watch.

"If the spider ever lets you pass..."

"Do not look behind you."

Mara swallowed.

She stepped onto the path.

The spider moved behind her.

She heard its legs touch the leaves.

One.

Two.

Three.

The cloaked figure walked ahead.

Mara kept her eyes forward.

The forest became darker.

The path became smaller.

Then she heard a familiar voice behind her.

"Mara, don't leave me."

She stopped.

It was her grandfather's voice.

The same voice she remembered from childhood.

Her body wanted to turn.

She almost did.

Then the cloaked figure stopped walking.

It raised one hand.

The forest became silent.

Mara understood.

The voice behind her was not her grandfather.

She kept walking.

The voice changed.

"Mara."

Now it sounded like her mother.

Then her brother.

Then her own voice.

Each one begged her to turn.

She refused.

At last, the path opened into a wide clearing.

The cloaked figure stood in the center.

A huge web covered the trees behind it.

Something was trapped inside the web.

Mara stepped closer.

The shape was small.

Human.

She raised her lamp again.

There was no flame.

Yet the lamp began to glow.

The light touched the web.

Mara saw a face.

Her own face.

The person trapped inside the web looked exactly like her.

The figure reached through the silk.

Its mouth opened.

"You finally found me."

Mara could not move.

The cloaked figure turned toward her.

For the first time, it removed its hood.

Mara stared at the face beneath it.

It was her grandfather.

But he had been dead for eleven years.

He smiled.

"Now you understand why I told you never to follow the silk."

Behind him, the giant spider slowly opened its mouth.

A new strand of silk fell toward Mara.

And written across it were three words.

"YOU ARE NEXT."
