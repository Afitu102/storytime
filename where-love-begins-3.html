Chapter 3 — The Truth She Never Knew

Amara could not move.

Daniel's words echoed inside her head.

"He's looking for you."

She stared at the narrow road behind them, expecting the mysterious figure to appear again.

But the path was empty.

Only the sound of leaves moving in the morning breeze could be heard.

"Who is he?" Amara finally asked.

Daniel looked at her carefully.

"I don't know his name."

Amara frowned.

"Then how do you know he's looking for me?"

Daniel took a slow breath.

"Because I've seen him before."

"Where?"

"Years ago."

Amara tightened her grip on the photograph.

"You keep saying things that make no sense."

Daniel looked at her with a mixture of sadness and concern.

"I know."

"Then tell me everything."

Daniel remained silent.

Amara stepped closer.

"You brought me here. You showed me a picture of my father. You told me he's alive. And now you're telling me someone is looking for me."

Her voice trembled.

"I deserve to know the truth."

Daniel nodded slowly.

"You do."

The Secret

Daniel looked around before speaking.

"Your father was not a poor man."

Amara looked confused.

"What does that have to do with anything?"

"Everything."

Daniel pointed at the photograph in her hands.

"Your father came from a powerful family."

Amara shook her head.

"My mother never told me that."

"Because she was afraid."

"Afraid of whom?"

Daniel hesitated.

"His family."

Amara stared at him.

"Why would my mother be afraid of his family?"

"Because they never wanted your mother to marry him."

Amara's eyes widened.

"They hated her?"

Daniel shook his head.

"They believed she was not good enough for him."

Amara looked away.

Suddenly, many things about her childhood began to feel different.

Her mother's constant fear.

The strange men who sometimes appeared around their house.

The questions her mother never answered.

The sudden decision to leave their old home.

Amara swallowed hard.

"So what happened to my father?"

Daniel looked at the ground.

"He disappeared."

"Because of his family?"

"Partly."

"What does that mean?"

Daniel looked at her.

"Your father discovered that someone close to his family was planning to have him killed."

Amara covered her mouth.

"Killed?"

Daniel nodded.

"He disappeared before they could find him."

"And my mother?"

"She believed they would come after you next."

Amara's eyes filled with tears.

"So she took me away?"

"Yes."

"And she made me believe my father had abandoned us."

Daniel nodded.

"She thought it was the only way to keep you safe."

Amara stared at the photograph again.

For years she had hated a man she had never met.

She had imagined him as someone who simply walked away from his family.

But now she wasn't sure what to believe anymore.

A Name From The Past

"Who was the person that wanted him dead?"

Daniel's expression became serious.

"His older brother."

Amara stared at him.

"His brother?"

"Yes."

"Why would his own brother want him dead?"

"Money."

Daniel paused.

"Your father was supposed to inherit everything."

Amara slowly shook her head.

"This sounds like a story."

"I wish it was."

Daniel reached into his leather bag again.

He brought out another folded piece of paper.

"Your mother wanted you to have this when you were old enough."

Amara took it with trembling fingers.

"What is it?"

"A letter."

"From my mother?"

"Yes."

Amara stared at the handwriting on the envelope.

She recognized it immediately.

Her mother's handwriting.

Tears rolled down her cheeks.

"Why didn't she give this to me herself?"

Daniel's voice softened.

"Because she knew she might not have enough time."

Amara closed her eyes.

For a moment, she could almost hear her mother's voice.

She opened the envelope.

Inside was a single sheet of paper.

Her hands shook as she unfolded it.

The Letter

"My dearest Amara..."

She stopped.

Those were the first words.

Her vision became blurry.

She wiped her tears and continued reading.

"If you are reading this, then Daniel has finally found you."

Amara looked up suddenly.

Daniel lowered his eyes.

She continued.

"I know you will have many questions."

"You may even be angry with me."

"But everything I did was to protect you."

Amara's lips trembled.

She read on.

"Your father did not abandon you."

Amara covered her mouth.

Tears fell onto the paper.

"He loved you before you were even born."

She stopped reading for a moment.

Her heart ached.

Then she continued.

"There are people who will do anything to prevent you from finding him."

"Do not trust everyone who tells you they know your father."

Amara looked at Daniel.

He remained silent.

The final sentence made her blood run cold.

"And most importantly, Amara..."

She read the words slowly.

"If Daniel tells you that your father is waiting for you, don't go to him."

Amara froze.

She slowly lowered the letter.

Her eyes moved toward Daniel.

"Why?"

Daniel's face had gone pale.

"Why would my mother tell me not to go with you?"

Daniel looked at the letter.

"Because she knew something I didn't."

Amara stepped backward.

"Then why did you bring me here?"

Daniel opened his mouth.

Before he could answer, they heard a sound behind them.

A branch snapped.

Both of them turned.

Someone was standing behind the trees.

Daniel immediately moved in front of Amara.

"Stay behind me."

Amara's heart raced.

A man slowly stepped out from the trees.

He was dressed in a dark shirt and trousers.

His face was unfamiliar.

But Daniel knew him.

"You shouldn't have come here," Daniel said.

The man smiled.

"Neither should you."

Amara looked from one man to the other.

"Who is he?"

Neither man answered.

The stranger looked directly at Amara.

"You look exactly like your mother."

Amara's heart skipped.

"How do you know my mother?"

The man smiled again.

"I knew her very well."

Daniel grabbed Amara's arm.

"We have to go."

But the man stepped forward.

"Before you leave, there is something Amara deserves to know."

Daniel stopped.

The man's eyes remained fixed on Amara.

"Your father isn't the only person who survived that night."

Amara's face became pale.

"What night?"

The man glanced at Daniel.

"The night your mother told everyone your father was dead."

Amara looked at Daniel.

"Is that true?"

Daniel said nothing.

The man's smile disappeared.

"Ask Daniel what happened that night."

Amara's eyes filled with fear.

"Daniel?"

Daniel finally looked at her.

And for the first time since she had met him, Amara saw something she had never seen before.

Fear.

"We need to leave now," Daniel whispered.

"Why?"

Daniel looked at the man.

Then he looked back at Amara.

"Because he was there that night."

Amara's heart pounded.

"And you were there too?"

Daniel closed his eyes for a moment.

When he opened them, tears were forming in his eyes.

"Yes."

Amara stepped away from him.

"What did you do?"

Daniel remained silent.

The man behind them smiled.

"Ask him who started the fire."

Amara stared at Daniel in disbelief.

"Daniel..."

He finally spoke.

"I didn't start the fire."

"Then who did?"

Daniel looked toward the man.

His voice dropped to a whisper.

"Your father's brother."

Suddenly, the man stepped closer.

"And now he knows you're alive."

Amara felt the world collapse beneath her.

Her father was alive.

Her mother had hidden the truth.

Daniel knew more than he had revealed.

And somewhere out there was a man who wanted her dead.

She looked at Daniel.

"What happens now?"

Daniel took her hand.

"Now we find your father before they do."

Amara hesitated.

"But my mother's letter told me not to trust you."

Daniel's eyes filled with sadness.

"Then you'll have to decide whether your mother was warning you about me..."

He paused.

"...or warning you about what I was forced to become."

Amara stared at him.

Before she could respond, a loud gunshot echoed from somewhere beyond the trees.

Amara screamed.

Daniel pulled her to the ground.

The mysterious man disappeared behind the trees.

Another gunshot followed.

Daniel grabbed Amara's hand.

"Run!"

They raced toward the village.

But neither of them knew that someone was already waiting for them there.

And that person was standing inside Amara's house.
