📜 The Demon Under the Veil

📜 Classic Authors • 🕒 30 min read

🌫 Chapter One – The Woman Nobody Knew

It was a cold evening.

The wind was blowing softly through the old streets.

People were rushing back to their homes before night became too dark.

There was one road everybody tried to avoid.

It was called Ash Street.

Nobody liked passing there after sunset.

The old houses looked empty.

The trees made strange sounds whenever the wind blew.

People always said something watched from the shadows.

Some laughed at the stories.

Some believed every word.

One old man always warned the children.

"If you ever see a woman wearing a white veil..."

"...do not stop."

"Do not talk to her."

"And never ask to see her face."

The children would laugh.

They thought it was just another old story.

One evening, a young man named Daniel arrived in the town.

He had come to work in the old library.

He did not know anything about Ash Street.

He did not know the stories people whispered at night.

Daniel was not someone who believed in ghosts.

He believed every strange thing had a simple answer.

That night, he worked late.

By the time he locked the library...

The streets were almost empty.

Only the sound of the wind could be heard.

Daniel looked around.

There were two roads home.

One was long.

The other was Ash Street.

He smiled.

"I'll save some time."

Without thinking twice...

He turned into Ash Street.

The air suddenly became colder.

Very cold.

Daniel stopped walking.

Something felt different.

The wind had stopped.

Everything was quiet.

Too quiet.

Then...

He heard footsteps.

Slow.

Soft.

Behind him.

Daniel turned around quickly.

Nobody was there.

He laughed at himself.

"You're imagining things."

He continued walking.

Again...

The footsteps returned.

Step...

Step...

Step...

This time they sounded closer.

Daniel turned around once more.

At the far end of the street...

A woman stood completely still.

She wore a long white dress.

A white veil covered her face.

She did not move.

She simply stood there...

Looking straight at him.

Daniel felt a chill run through his body.

For the first time in his life...

He wished he had taken the longer road.

🌙 End of Chapter One

👁 Chapter Two – The Woman Who Came Closer

Daniel stood still.

His eyes stayed on the woman.

She had not moved.

The white veil still covered her face.

The street was silent.

Even the wind had disappeared.

Daniel rubbed his eyes.

"It must be someone walking home."

He tried to calm himself.

He took one step forward.

The woman did not move.

He took another step.

Still...

She remained standing.

Daniel smiled a little.

Maybe the old stories were only stories after all.

Then...

He looked down for only a second.

When he looked up again...

The woman was much closer.

Daniel froze.

His heart began beating fast.

"No..."

"She couldn't have walked that fast."

There had been no footsteps.

No sound.

Nothing.

Yet she was now standing only a few houses away.

Daniel slowly stepped backward.

The woman remained still.

Her head was slightly lowered.

The long white veil moved gently in the cold air.

Daniel turned around.

He decided to leave.

He walked faster.

Soon...

He was almost running.

After a few minutes, he finally stopped.

He looked behind him.

Nobody.

The street was empty.

Daniel breathed deeply.

"I knew it."

"I scared myself."

He laughed quietly.

Then...

A soft voice came from behind him.

"Are you leaving already?"

Daniel's whole body became cold.

Very slowly...

He turned around.

The woman was standing right behind him.

So close...

He could almost touch the edge of her veil.

Daniel jumped back in fear.

The woman did not move.

She simply lifted one hand.

A pale hand.

She pointed toward the end of the street.

"Come with me."

Daniel shook his head.

"No..."

"I don't know you."

The woman gave no answer.

She slowly turned around.

Then she began walking away.

Step...

Step...

Step...

After a few seconds...

She disappeared into the thick fog.

Daniel stood there, breathing heavily.

He wanted to run home.

He really did.

But something inside him kept asking one question.

"Who is she..."

"...and why did she call me?"

Without thinking...

Daniel slowly followed her into the fog.
