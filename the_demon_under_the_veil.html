📜 The Demon Under the Veil

📜 Classic Authors • 🕒 30 min read

🌫 Chapter One – The Woman Nobody Knew

It was a cold evening.

The wind was blowing softly through the old streets.

People were rushing back to their homes before night became too dark.

There was one road everybody tried to avoid.

It was called Ash Street.

Nobody liked passing there after sunset.

The old houses looked empty.

The trees made strange sounds whenever the wind blew.

People always said something watched from the shadows.

Some laughed at the stories.

Some believed every word.

One old man always warned the children.

"If you ever see a woman wearing a white veil..."

"...do not stop."

"Do not talk to her."

"And never ask to see her face."

The children would laugh.

They thought it was just another old story.

One evening, a young man named Daniel arrived in the town.

He had come to work in the old library.

He did not know anything about Ash Street.

He did not know the stories people whispered at night.

Daniel was not someone who believed in ghosts.

He believed every strange thing had a simple answer.

That night, he worked late.

By the time he locked the library...

The streets were almost empty.

Only the sound of the wind could be heard.

Daniel looked around.

There were two roads home.

One was long.

The other was Ash Street.

He smiled.

"I'll save some time."

Without thinking twice...

He turned into Ash Street.

The air suddenly became colder.

Very cold.

Daniel stopped walking.

Something felt different.

The wind had stopped.

Everything was quiet.

Too quiet.

Then...

He heard footsteps.

Slow.

Soft.

Behind him.

Daniel turned around quickly.

Nobody was there.

He laughed at himself.

"You're imagining things."

He continued walking.

Again...

The footsteps returned.

Step...

Step...

Step...

This time they sounded closer.

Daniel turned around once more.

At the far end of the street...

A woman stood completely still.

She wore a long white dress.

A white veil covered her face.

She did not move.

She simply stood there...

Looking straight at him.

Daniel felt a chill run through his body.

For the first time in his life...

He wished he had taken the longer road.

🌙 End of Chapter One

👁 Chapter Two – The Woman Who Came Closer

Daniel stood still.

His eyes stayed on the woman.

She had not moved.

The white veil still covered her face.

The street was silent.

Even the wind had disappeared.

Daniel rubbed his eyes.

"It must be someone walking home."

He tried to calm himself.

He took one step forward.

The woman did not move.

He took another step.

Still...

She remained standing.

Daniel smiled a little.

Maybe the old stories were only stories after all.

Then...

He looked down for only a second.

When he looked up again...

The woman was much closer.

Daniel froze.

His heart began beating fast.

"No..."

"She couldn't have walked that fast."

There had been no footsteps.

No sound.

Nothing.

Yet she was now standing only a few houses away.

Daniel slowly stepped backward.

The woman remained still.

Her head was slightly lowered.

The long white veil moved gently in the cold air.

Daniel turned around.

He decided to leave.

He walked faster.

Soon...

He was almost running.

After a few minutes, he finally stopped.

He looked behind him.

Nobody.

The street was empty.

Daniel breathed deeply.

"I knew it."

"I scared myself."

He laughed quietly.

Then...

A soft voice came from behind him.

"Are you leaving already?"

Daniel's whole body became cold.

Very slowly...

He turned around.

The woman was standing right behind him.

So close...

He could almost touch the edge of her veil.

Daniel jumped back in fear.

The woman did not move.

She simply lifted one hand.

A pale hand.

She pointed toward the end of the street.

"Come with me."

Daniel shook his head.

"No..."

"I don't know you."

The woman gave no answer.

She slowly turned around.

Then she began walking away.

Step...

Step...

Step...

After a few seconds...

She disappeared into the thick fog.

Daniel stood there, breathing heavily.

He wanted to run home.

He really did.

But something inside him kept asking one question.

"Who is she..."

"...and why did she call me?"

Without thinking...

Daniel slowly followed her into the fog.

🌙 End of Chapter Two

🏚 Chapter Three – The House in the Fog

The fog was thick.

Daniel could hardly see where he was going.

The woman never looked back.

She just kept walking.

Slowly.

Quietly.

Daniel followed her.

He did not know why.

Something inside him kept saying,

"Just a little farther."

The houses around him slowly disappeared.

Soon...

He was no longer on Ash Street.

He was standing in front of an old house.

It was bigger than every other house in town.

Its walls were cracked.

The windows were broken.

The front gate hung on one hinge.

It looked like nobody had lived there for many years.

The woman stopped.

She stood in front of the old wooden door.

Without touching it...

The door slowly opened by itself.

Creeeeak...

Daniel felt his legs become weak.

He wanted to turn around.

He wanted to run.

But the road behind him was gone.

Only thick fog remained.

"What's happening?"

The woman stepped inside.

The house became dark again.

Daniel stood outside.

His heart beat louder than ever.

He looked around.

Nobody.

Not even a bird.

Only silence.

Then...

A soft cry came from inside the house.

It sounded like someone was asking for help.

"Please..."

"Help me..."

Daniel swallowed hard.

It sounded like a young woman.

He slowly stepped through the doorway.

The air inside was freezing cold.

Dust covered everything.

Old chairs lay on the floor.

Pictures hung crooked on the walls.

A large clock stood in the corner.

Its hands had stopped.

Twelve o'clock.

Daniel looked around.

The veiled woman was gone.

She had disappeared.

Only her footsteps remained.

Step...

Step...

Step...

They came from upstairs.

Daniel slowly walked toward the staircase.

The wooden steps made loud sounds.

Creak...

Creak...

Halfway up...

He noticed something on the wall.

It was a family picture.

Everyone in the picture was smiling.

Except one woman.

Her face had been scratched away.

Only the white veil remained.

Daniel stared at it.

Then he heard someone whisper right beside his ear.

"You shouldn't have come..."

Daniel turned around immediately.

Nobody was there.

But when he looked back at the picture...

The woman no longer had a scratched face.

She was smiling at him.

A smile that was never there before.

🌙 End of Chapter Three

🕯 Chapter Four – The Smile in the Mirror

Daniel could not move.

The woman in the picture was smiling at him.

Just a few seconds ago...

Her face had been scratched away.

Now...

She was looking straight at him.

Daniel slowly stepped back.

His hands began shaking.

"This isn't real..."

"It can't be real..."

Suddenly...

The picture fell from the wall.

Bang!

The sound echoed through the whole house.

Daniel jumped.

His breathing became faster.

Then...

He heard footsteps again.

Slow.

Heavy.

They were coming from the end of the hallway.

Daniel looked up.

The veiled woman was standing there.

She was not moving.

She simply stood there.

Looking at him.

Daniel gathered courage.

He spoke.

"Who are you?"

No answer.

Only silence.

He tried again.

"What do you want from me?"

The woman slowly lifted one finger.

She pointed toward a door.

It was the last door in the hallway.

Then...

She disappeared.

Just like smoke.

Daniel blinked.

She was gone.

Only the old door remained.

He slowly walked toward it.

The closer he got...

The colder the air became.

He placed his hand on the door handle.

It was freezing.

Very slowly...

He pushed the door open.

Inside was an old bedroom.

Everything was covered with dust.

A broken chair stood near the window.

An old bed filled one corner.

There was also a large mirror.

It reached almost from the floor to the ceiling.

Daniel walked closer.

He looked into it.

He saw himself.

Nothing strange.

He sighed with relief.

Then...

His reflection smiled.

Daniel did not.

His body froze.

Slowly...

His reflection raised one hand.

Daniel's hand stayed down.

The reflection was moving on its own.

Then the reflection whispered,

"Turn around..."

Daniel's heart almost stopped.

Very slowly...

He turned around.

Standing behind him...

Was the veiled woman.

Only this time...

She was close enough to touch him.

🌙 End of Chapter Four

👣 Chapter Five – The Face Behind the Veil

Daniel could not breathe.

The woman was standing so close.

He could hear her breathing.

Cold.

Slow.

She raised one hand.

It was pale.

Her fingers looked almost like dry branches.

Daniel wanted to run.

But his legs refused to move.

The woman pointed at the mirror.

Daniel slowly looked back.

This time...

The mirror was empty.

His own reflection had disappeared.

"No..."

Daniel whispered.

The woman took one more step.

Very slowly...

She reached for the veil.

Daniel closed his eyes.

He did not want to see what was hiding underneath.

But something inside him said,

"Look..."

He opened his eyes.

The veil was gone.

Daniel gasped.

The woman had no face.

Only darkness.

A deep black shadow where her face should have been.

No eyes.

No nose.

No mouth.

Just darkness.

Then...

Two glowing red eyes slowly opened inside the darkness.

Daniel stumbled backward.

He fell to the floor.

The woman did not move.

Her voice filled the room.

It did not sound human.

It sounded like many voices speaking together.

"You were never meant to come here."

Daniel shook his head.

"I didn't know..."

"I only followed you."

The room suddenly became darker.

The windows closed by themselves.

The old door slammed shut.

Bang!

Daniel jumped.

He rushed to the door.

He pulled the handle.

It would not open.

He pushed harder.

Nothing.

The woman slowly walked toward him.

Every step echoed inside the room.

Step...

Step...

Step...

Daniel looked around for another way out.

Then he noticed something.

On the small table beside the bed...

There was an old diary.

Its cover was covered in dust.

Across the front were three faded words.

"DO NOT OPEN."

The woman suddenly stopped walking.

For the first time...

She seemed afraid.

Very afraid.

Daniel looked from the diary...

Back to the woman.

He slowly reached for it.

The woman screamed.

A loud scream that shook the whole house.

The moment Daniel touched the diary...

Every candle in the room went out.

The room became completely dark.

🌙 End of Chapter Five

⚰ Chapter Six – The Diary's Secret

The room was dark.

Very dark.

Daniel could not see his own hands.

The woman's scream still echoed through the house.

Then...

The old diary began to glow.

Not brightly.

Just enough for Daniel to see it.

Its old pages slowly opened by themselves.

The wind turned each page.

One...

Two...

Three...

Until it stopped.

There was only one sentence written across the page.

"She was never the demon."

Daniel frowned.

He read it again.

"She was never the demon."

Before he could think...

More words slowly appeared on the page.

Like someone was writing them.

"The real demon wears no veil."

Daniel looked up.

The veiled woman was standing at the other end of the room.

She was no longer walking toward him.

She looked...

Afraid.

Suddenly...

A loud laugh filled the room.

It did not come from the woman.

It came from everywhere.

The walls shook.

The floor cracked.

The mirror exploded into hundreds of tiny pieces.

Daniel covered his face.

When he looked again...

A tall black shadow stood behind the woman.

It had no feet.

It floated above the floor.

Its eyes burned like fire.

Its smile stretched from one side of its face to the other.

The woman quickly stepped in front of Daniel.

She raised both hands.

For the first time...

She spoke clearly.

"Run!"

Daniel stared at her.

He was confused.

She was protecting him.

The black shadow laughed again.

"After all these years..."

"...you still protect strangers."

The woman did not answer.

She stood her ground.

The shadow lifted one hand.

Dark smoke filled the room.

The house began to shake harder.

Pieces of the ceiling started falling.

Daniel looked at the diary.

Another sentence appeared.

"Only the truth can break the curse."

Daniel whispered,

"What truth?"

But before anyone could answer...

The floor beneath them suddenly split open.

A deep hole appeared.

The black shadow slowly turned its burning eyes toward Daniel.

Then it smiled.

"Now..."

"...it's your turn."
