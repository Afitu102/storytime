🧚 Whispers of the Enchanted Forest

📚 Fairy Tales • 🕒 25 min read

🌿 Chapter One – The Girl Beneath the Silver Tree

The moon was already high when Elara reached the edge of the forest.

She carried a small blue lantern in one hand.

Its flame was strange.

It gave no heat.

It only glowed.

Behind her was the village.

Ahead was the forest.

The trees stood close together, their branches joining above the path like a giant roof.

Elara took one slow step forward.

A soft sound came from somewhere between the trees.

Ting...

She stopped.

Ting...

It sounded like a tiny bell.

She raised the lantern.

Small golden lights floated between the branches.

Fireflies.

But these were different.

Each one carried a tiny blue spark inside its body.

Elara smiled.

"So the stories were true."

The fireflies moved away from the path.

They formed a glowing line through the trees.

Elara followed.

The deeper she walked, the quieter the forest became.

Her footsteps became the only sound.

Then she heard water.

A small stream appeared between two large rocks.

Elara crossed it carefully.

On the other side stood a tree covered in silver leaves.

Its trunk was wide.

Its branches reached far above the forest.

At the base of the tree was a little wooden door.

Elara moved closer.

There was a symbol carved into it.

A crescent moon surrounded by seven stars.

She touched the symbol.

The blue lantern suddenly became brighter.

The door opened.

Elara stepped back.

Warm golden light spilled onto the grass.

A small room appeared inside the tree.

There was a round table.

Three wooden chairs.

And a glass bottle filled with glowing dust.

Beside the bottle was a folded piece of paper.

Elara picked it up.

Her hands trembled when she saw the name written on it.

"Elara."

She slowly unfolded the paper.

Only one sentence was written inside.

"Do not trust the girl wearing a crown of flowers."

Elara stared at the words.

She had never heard of such a girl.

Then something moved behind her.

A soft step on the grass.

Elara turned.

A young girl stood beneath the silver tree.

She wore a long green dress.

Flowers covered her hair.

A small golden crown rested on her head.

The girl smiled.

"Elara..."

Elara's heart stopped for a moment.

The girl lifted her hand.

A blue butterfly landed on her finger.

"You finally found me."

Elara looked down at the paper.

Then back at the girl.

The sentence on the paper suddenly changed.

New words slowly appeared.

"RUN BEFORE SHE REMEMBERS YOUR FACE."
