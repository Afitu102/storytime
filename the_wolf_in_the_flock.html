🐺 The Wolf in the Flock

🐾 Animal Story • 🕒 25 min read

🐺 Chapter One – The New Sheep

The morning sun was just coming out.

The grass was still wet.

The sheep slowly walked out of their wooden pen.

Some were eating.

Some were running around.

Little lambs chased one another happily.

Life on the farm was peaceful.

Old Shepherd Musa stood beside the gate with his walking stick.

Every morning he counted his sheep before leading them to the green field.

He never missed a single one.

"One..."

"Two..."

"Three..."

He smiled.

Everything looked normal.

Or so he thought.

Far away, behind the tall trees, two yellow eyes watched the sheep.

A hungry wolf hid inside the bushes.

He had not eaten for many days.

His stomach growled loudly.

He looked at the flock.

There were many fat sheep.

But there was a problem.

The shepherd's dogs never left the flock.

They were big.

Strong.

And very fast.

Every time the wolf came close...

The dogs chased him away.

The wolf sighed.

"I need another plan."

He looked around the forest.

Then he saw something hanging on a broken fence.

It was old sheep wool.

Someone had thrown it away after cutting a sheep's hair.

The wolf slowly smiled.

A clever smile.

"I don't need to chase them..."

"I'll become one of them."

He quietly picked up the dirty white wool.

He wrapped it around himself.

From far away...

He no longer looked like a wolf.

He looked like another sheep.

Not perfect.

But close enough.

That evening, when the shepherd opened the gate for the sheep to return home...

The wolf quietly walked in with them.

Nobody noticed.

Not the shepherd.

Not the dogs.

Not even the sheep.

Only one tiny lamb looked at the new sheep for a little longer.

His name was Kito.

He tilted his head.

Something did not feel right.

The new sheep never smiled.

It never made any sound.

And its eyes...

They looked nothing like sheep eyes.

They looked hungry.

Very hungry.

Kito quietly whispered to himself,

"I think someone here is pretending..."

🌙 End of Chapter One

🌲 Chapter Two – The Sheep That Never Ate

The next morning, the sun rose slowly over the hills.

The birds began singing.

The shepherd opened the gate.

The sheep happily ran outside.

They rushed straight to the fresh green grass.

Every sheep lowered its head and began eating.

Every sheep...

Except one.

The new sheep simply stood there.

It looked around.

Its eyes were not on the grass.

Its eyes were on the little lambs.

Kito quietly watched from a short distance.

He frowned.

"That's strange."

He walked closer.

The new sheep quickly bent down and pretended to eat.

But Kito noticed something.

The grass under its mouth never moved.

It was only acting.

Kito whispered,

"Real sheep eat."

"This one doesn't."

He wanted to tell the others.

So he ran to his best friend, a playful lamb named Bina.

"Bina, something is wrong with the new sheep."

Bina laughed.

"You worry too much."

"It's just shy."

Kito shook his head.

"No."

"I've been watching it."

"It doesn't eat."

"It doesn't play."

"It only watches us."

Bina looked at the strange sheep.

For a moment...

She also became quiet.

"It does look... different."

Just then, one of the shepherd's dogs walked past.

The wolf froze.

He lowered his head.

The dog sniffed the air.

He stopped.

He looked at the new sheep.

The wolf's heart began beating fast.

Very fast.

The dog took one step closer.

Then another.

The wolf was ready to run.

But before the dog reached him...

The shepherd whistled.

"Bruno!"

"Come here!"

The dog turned around and ran back to the shepherd.

The wolf quietly breathed out.

He had escaped.

For now.

That evening, the sheep returned home.

As everyone settled down to sleep...

The wolf stayed awake.

His stomach growled loudly.

He looked across the pen.

The little lambs were sleeping close together.

Nobody was watching.

He slowly smiled.

"Tonight..."

"...I finally eat."

But hidden behind a pile of hay...

Kito was still awake.

And he was watching everything.

🌙 End of Chapter Two

🌙 Chapter Three – Eyes in the Dark

Night covered the farm.

The moon was bright.

The sheep were asleep.

Only the sound of crickets could be heard.

Kito hid behind a pile of hay.

He tried not to make any sound.

His little heart was beating very fast.

He kept watching the new sheep.

For a long time...

Nothing happened.

Then...

The strange sheep slowly stood up.

It looked around carefully.

Left.

Right.

Nobody seemed to be awake.

Slowly, it walked away from the sleeping sheep.

When it reached the darkest corner of the pen...

It pulled off the white wool.

Kito almost screamed.

It was not a sheep.

It was the wolf.

His sharp teeth shined in the moonlight.

His yellow eyes looked even scarier at night.

The wolf stretched his legs and smiled.

"I'm tired of pretending."

He slowly walked toward the sleeping lambs.

Each step was quiet.

Very quiet.

Kito wanted to warn everyone.

But he was afraid.

If the wolf saw him...

He would be the first to disappear.

The wolf stopped beside little Bina.

She was sleeping peacefully.

The wolf opened his mouth.

Just then...

A loud owl cried from the tree above.

"Hoooo!"

The wolf quickly looked up.

At the same moment...

Bruno, the shepherd's biggest dog, lifted his head.

He sniffed the air.

His nose twitched.

He smelled something.

Not sheep.

Wolf.

The wolf quickly pulled the white wool back over himself.

He lay down before Bruno reached him.

The dog slowly walked through the pen.

He sniffed every sheep.

When he reached the wolf...

He stopped.

His ears stood straight.

His tail became stiff.

The wolf closed his eyes and pretended to sleep.

Bruno growled softly.

"Grrrr..."

The shepherd's voice suddenly came from inside the house.

"Bruno!"

"Quiet!"

The dog looked one more time at the strange sheep.

Then he slowly walked away.

The wolf opened one eye.

He smiled again.

"That dog is getting suspicious."

Kito finally understood.

Nobody would believe him without proof.

He had to find a way to show everyone the truth.

Before the wolf hurt someone.

🌙 End of Chapter Three
